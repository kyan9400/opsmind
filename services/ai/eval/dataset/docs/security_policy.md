# Information Security Policy

Policy SEC-04. Owner: Security officer. Mandatory for employees and contractors.

## Accounts and passwords

- Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN, GitHub and every admin console.
- Passwords are at least 14 characters and are stored only in the company password manager. Never reuse a work password on another site.
- Shared accounts are forbidden. Every person gets a named account.

## Devices

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and lock the screen after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal phones may be used for work email only with the mobile device management profile installed.

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to security@volgahome.example within 1 hour, so it can be wiped remotely.

## Phishing

Use the "Report phishing" button in the mail client for suspicious emails. Do not forward them to colleagues. The security team runs a phishing simulation every quarter; people who click take a short refresher course.

## Access reviews

Managers review the access rights of their team every quarter. Access for people who leave the company is revoked on their last working day, before 18:00.

## Data classification

| Class | Examples | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Public | product pages, press releases | no restrictions |
| Internal | policies, sprint plans | employees only |
| Confidential | customer orders, salaries | need-to-know, encrypted in transit |
| Restricted | payment data, passwords, device location history | named people only, logged access |

Restricted data must never be copied to personal devices or pasted into external chat or translation tools.
