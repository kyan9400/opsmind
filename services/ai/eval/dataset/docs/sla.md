# Volga Cloud Service Level Agreement

Document SLA-3. Applies to paid Volga Cloud plans. The free Basic plan has no uptime commitment.

## Availability

We commit to a monthly availability of 99.5% for the Plus plan and 99.9% for the Pro plan. Availability is measured every minute by external probes against the app API and the device gateway.

## Service credits

If availability falls below the commitment, the customer receives a credit on the next invoice:

| Monthly availability | Credit |
|---|---|
| below the commitment, at or above 99.0% | 10% of the monthly fee |
| below 99.0% | 25% of the monthly fee |

Credits must be requested within 30 days after the end of the month. Credits are the only compensation for downtime.

## Support response times

| Priority | Example | Pro | Plus |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | the app or all devices are offline | 1 hour, 24/7 | 4 hours |
| P2 | one feature does not work | 4 business hours | 1 business day |
| P3 | question or feature request | 2 business days | 3 business days |

## Maintenance

Planned maintenance happens on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 Moscow time and is announced at least 72 hours in advance. Planned maintenance does not count as downtime.

## Exclusions

Outages caused by the customer's home internet, mobile operator, power failures or devices without the latest firmware are excluded.
