# New Employee Onboarding Guide

Guide ONB-01. Owner: People team. Welcome to Volga Home!

## Before your first day

Your manager opens an IT ticket of type ONB-1 at least 5 business days before you start. IT prepares your laptop (MacBook Air or ThinkPad T14, your choice), your email and your chat account. You receive a welcome email with the office address in Kazan, the start time and the name of your onboarding buddy.

## First day

- 10:00: meet your buddy at reception and collect your access badge.
- Sign the employment documents with HR and read the Leave Policy and the Expense and Travel Policy.
- Set up multi-factor authentication and the password manager with IT before lunch.
- Lunch with your team is paid by the company.

## First week

Complete the mandatory online courses in the learning portal: information security basics, personal data protection (152-FZ) and, for warehouse staff, occupational safety. Engineers also get access to the on-call handbook but join the rotation only after three months.

Your buddy answers everyday questions: where things are, who owns what, how to book a meeting room. They are not your manager and do not evaluate your work.

## Probation and check-ins

Probation lasts three months. You and your manager agree on goals in week one and meet for check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days. At the 90-day meeting the manager confirms the end of probation or, rarely, extends the conversation with HR.

## Equipment

Laptops are replaced every three years. Extra monitors, keyboards and headsets are ordered through the IT portal and do not need expense approval. Returning employees keep their old equipment only if IT agrees.
