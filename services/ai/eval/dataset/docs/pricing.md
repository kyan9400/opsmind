## Volga Cloud plans

- Basic: free. One home, 7 days of event history, email alerts.
- Plus: 299 RUB per month. Up to 3 homes, 90 days of history, push and SMS alerts, shared access for family members.
- Pro: 699 RUB per month. Unlimited homes, 1 year of history, open API access, priority support.

Paying yearly gives two months free: Plus costs 2,990 RUB and Pro 6,990 RUB per year.

## Discounts

- Business customers buying 50 or more devices in one order get 10% off devices. Cloud plans are not discounted.
- Promo codes cannot be combined; one code per order.
- Price match: if a partner store sells the same device cheaper, support refunds the difference within 7 days of purchase.
