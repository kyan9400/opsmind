# Refund and Returns Policy

Policy POL-FIN-03. Owner: Finance. Applies to all orders placed on volgahome.example and to Volga Cloud subscriptions.

## Devices

Unopened devices can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund, including the original delivery fee. Opened devices in working order can be returned within 14 days minus a 10% restocking fee. The Thermo One TH-100 and the Leak Sensor LS-20 must be returned with their mounting kit.

A device that stops working within the 12-month warranty is repaired or replaced, never refunded in cash. The customer opens a warranty claim and support issues a prepaid return label.

## Volga Cloud subscriptions

Monthly and annual Volga Cloud plans can be cancelled for a full refund within 30 days of the first payment. After 30 days an annual plan is refunded pro rata for the unused full months, as store credit only. Renewals are not refundable once the new period has started.

## How to process a refund

1. Support fills in form RF-2 in the billing console and attaches the order number (format ORD-NNNNNNN).
2. Refunds up to 50,000 RUB are approved by the support team lead. Larger refunds need sign-off from the Finance controller.
3. Card refunds reach the customer within 5 to 10 business days. Refunds by bank transfer are paid within 3 business days of approval.

Money is only ever returned to the card or account that paid for the order. This is an anti-fraud control and support cannot override it.

## Chargebacks

If a customer disputes a payment with their bank instead of asking us, the order is put on hold until the dispute is resolved. Support must answer the payment provider within 7 days using the evidence template CB-1.
