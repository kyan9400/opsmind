# Shipping Policy

Policy POL-OPS-07. Owner: Operations. All orders ship from our warehouse in Kazan.

## Dispatch times

Orders paid before 14:00 Moscow time on a business day leave the warehouse the same day. Orders paid later, or at weekends and on public holidays, leave on the next business day. Pre-orders ship on the date shown on the product page.

## Delivery options

- Standard delivery by CDEK or Russian Post: 2 to 5 business days to most regions, up to 12 days to the Far East.
- Express delivery (service code SHP-EXP): next business day in Moscow, Saint Petersburg and Kazan only. Express is not available to post office boxes or parcel lockers.
- Pickup from the Kazan showroom: ready within 2 hours during opening hours.

Standard delivery is free for orders over 5,000 RUB. Below that the fee is 390 RUB. Express costs 790 RUB regardless of the order value.

## Tracking

Customers receive a tracking link by SMS and email as soon as the carrier scans the parcel. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first event.

## Damaged or lost parcels

A parcel that arrives damaged must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with photos of the box and the device. We send a replacement before the carrier investigation is finished.

If tracking shows no movement for 7 days, support opens a search request with the carrier. A parcel is declared lost after 14 days without movement, and the customer chooses between a replacement and a full refund.

We do not ship outside Russia yet. Addresses in Belarus and Kazakhstan are on the roadmap for 2027.
