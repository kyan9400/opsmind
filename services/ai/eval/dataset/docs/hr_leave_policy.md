# Leave Policy

HR policy HR-POL-02. Applies to all employees on an employment contract with Volga Home LLC.

## Annual leave

Every employee has 28 calendar days of paid annual leave per year. Leave accrues from the first day of employment; after six months you may take the full amount. At least one part of your leave each year must be 14 calendar days or longer.

Request annual leave with form HR-12 in the HR portal at least 14 days before the first day of leave. Your line manager approves or suggests other dates within 3 business days. Holiday pay is transferred no later than 3 days before the leave starts.

Up to 7 unused days can be carried over, and they must be taken by the end of March of the following year. Days beyond that are not paid out while you remain employed.

## Sick leave

If you are ill, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. Submit form HR-21 in the HR portal with the number of your electronic sick note once the doctor closes it. The first three days of sick pay are paid by the company, the rest by the Social Fund.

## Other leave

- Parental leave follows Russian labour law; HR prepares the documents, so contact HR at least one month before the expected date.
- Unpaid leave: up to 5 days per calendar year with manager approval, requested with form HR-12.
- Two extra paid days for your wedding, the birth of a child or the death of a close relative.

Public holidays are set by the production calendar published by HR in December.
