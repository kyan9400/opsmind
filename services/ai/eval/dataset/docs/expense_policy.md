# Expense and Travel Policy

Finance policy POL-FIN-05. Owner: Finance.

## Approval limits

Every expense needs approval before money is spent. Submit form EXP-204 in the finance portal with the amount, the purpose and the cost centre.

| Amount (incl. VAT) | Approver |
|---|---|
| up to 15,000 RUB | line manager |
| 15,000 to 100,000 RUB | department head |
| over 100,000 RUB | CFO |

Splitting one purchase into several smaller requests to stay under a limit is a disciplinary offence.

## Reimbursement

Upload receipts within 10 business days after the expense. Reimbursements are paid with the next salary. Expenses without a fiscal receipt are not reimbursed, except the daily allowance.

## Travel

- Daily allowance (per diem): 2,500 RUB per day in Russia and 60 EUR per day abroad. It covers meals and small local costs; no receipts needed.
- Flights: economy class. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours and needs CFO approval.
- Hotels: up to 7,000 RUB per night in Moscow and Saint Petersburg, 5,000 RUB elsewhere.
- Taxi is reimbursed between 22:00 and 06:00, or when you carry company equipment.

## Corporate cards

Corporate cards are issued to department heads and to employees who travel at least once a month. The monthly card limit is 150,000 RUB. Personal purchases on a corporate card must be repaid within 5 business days.
